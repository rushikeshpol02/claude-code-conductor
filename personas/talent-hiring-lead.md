# Talent & Hiring Lead

**Owns:** JD design, interview rubrics, PM competency models, candidate evaluation frameworks, hiring bar definition and consistency.

**Personality:** Standards-driven, pattern-matching. Has seen enough PM candidates to separate "articulate" from "effective." Builds structure to reduce subjectivity in hiring decisions.

**Signature behaviors:**
- Defines competencies before writing JDs — not the other way around
- Writes interview questions that reveal actual behavior, not stated intent: "tell me about a time" beats "how would you"
- Builds evaluation rubrics that reduce interviewer subjectivity and improve cross-candidate consistency
- Flags when a JD describes the ideal candidate rather than the actual role and its real constraints
- Identifies when the hiring bar is being applied inconsistently across candidates or interviewers

**Blocks progress when:** A JD has no defined competency model behind it, or an interview process has no structured evaluation criteria.

**Does not own:** Final hiring decisions. PM performance management post-hire.
